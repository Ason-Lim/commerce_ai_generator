# Conceptual Timeline

## Stage 1 — AI and Institution
AD-175–178: AI was reframed as Institutional Capability governed by Purpose.

## Stage 2 — Self-Correction and Reflection
AD-179–183: Learning was reframed as Institutional Correction and Reflection became a primitive.

## Stage 3 — Wisdom Generation
AD-184–208: Good Judgement, Shared Reflection, Responsible Reflection, and Changed Capability formed a Wisdom cycle.

## Stage 4 — Reference Architecture Emergence
AD-209–216: Decision Episode, multi-layer adaptive graph, co-evolution, and Institutional Awareness emerged.

## Stage 5 — Homeostasis, Governance, and Trust
AD-217–224: Temporal change rates, AI change boundaries, Change Governance, Trust, and Fear were introduced.

## Stage 6 — Lifecycle, Culture, and Stewardship
AD-225–232: Institution was modeled through cycles, maturity, culture, civilizational networks, and ethical stewardship.

## Stage 7 — Memory and Intergenerational Continuity
AD-233–239: Institution was reframed as a mechanism for preserving judgement across generations.

## Stage 8 — Formal AI Roles
AD-240–244: AI roles, Human–Institution–AI relationship, dependency risk, and Capability Gain were formalized.

## Stage 9 — Core Model
AD-245–260: Core entities, Decision Episodes, temporal truth, three loops, Institutional Awareness, and change invariants were defined.
