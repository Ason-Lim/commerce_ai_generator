## 6. Feedback Evaluation Subject / Source Boundary

Existing provider-evidence infrastructure provides a reusable opaque
evaluation-subject reference.

It may preserve that multiple evidence items concern the same bounded
evaluation subject without manufacturing canonical provider identity.

Therefore:

`Feedback Subject Reference != Canonical Provider Identity`

Existing source-relationship semantics may also preserve whether evidence is
subject-supplied, third-party, internal observation or unknown where those
meanings are applicable.

However:

`Source Relationship != Verification`

`Source Relationship != Trust`

`Source Relationship != Feedback Authenticity`

Feedback evidence source identity is directly preservable through existing
provenance substrate.

Feedback-author identity is not the same concept.

The source of an evidence record may be known while the identity or authority
of the person who authored customer feedback remains unresolved.

Therefore the R4-U1 dependency is interpreted narrowly:

`R4-U1 Feedback Author Identity / Reference = UNRESOLVED`

This does not make feedback evidence source identity unresolved.

## 7. Experience Correlation Boundary

Existing logistics evidence provides reusable correlation references,
including where supplied:

- shipment, piece or package scope;
- scope reference;
- carrier reference;
- tracking number;
- source record reference;
- request correlation reference.

These references may support bounded feedback-to-experience correlation.

They do not establish customer verification.

Therefore:

`Shipment Correlation != Customer Verification`

`Tracking Correlation != Customer Verification`

`Experience Correlation != Verified Customer Experience`

`Correlation != Feedback Eligibility`

A future feedback-to-experience eligibility contract may need to determine
whether the author, provider/service, shipment or other bounded experience
references are sufficiently related for a specific verification purpose.

`R4-U3 Feedback-to-Experience Eligibility = UNRESOLVED`

## 8. Feedback Author / Verified Experience Boundary

Selected evidence does not establish a canonical feedback-author identity
contract.

A future author-reference contract may preserve an opaque author/customer
reference without requiring R4 to own canonical customer identity.

Selected evidence also does not establish that a feedback author actually
experienced the relevant provider or service.

Therefore:

`Author Reference != Canonical Customer Identity`

`Author Reference != Verified Customer Experience`

`Experience Correlation != Verified Customer Experience`

Verified customer experience requires separate authoritative evidence.

`R4-U2 Verified Customer Experience Authority = UNRESOLVED_EXTERNAL`

Even if experience is later verified:

`Verified Experience != Positive Feedback`

`Verified Experience != Feedback Truth`

Verification establishes only the bounded verification claim supported by its
evidence. It does not establish the objective correctness of every statement
made by the customer.

---

## 9. Feedback Time Boundary

R4 distinguishes multiple temporal concepts.

Existing provenance can directly preserve retrieval time.

Existing effective time may be reused only where its meaning is explicitly
compatible with the feedback evidence.

It must not be silently reinterpreted as customer experience time or feedback
submission time.

Therefore:

`Effective Time != Experience Time`

`Effective Time != Feedback Submission Time`

`Retrieval Time != Feedback Submission Time`

A future feedback contract may need distinct fields for:

- experience time;
- feedback submission/report time;
- retrieval time;
- effective time where applicable.

`R4-U4 Feedback Experience Time = UNRESOLVED`

Feedback submission/report-time semantics also require a new feedback-specific
contract.

## 12. Customer-Reported Incident Boundary

Customer feedback may contain reports of operational experiences such as
delay, damage, loss or poor tracking.

Those reports may eventually be preserved as customer-reported claims.

They must not be silently promoted into canonical operational facts.

Therefore:

`Customer-Reported Delay != Canonical Delay`

`Customer-Reported Damage != Canonical Damage`

`Customer-Reported Loss != Canonical Loss`

Likewise:

`Customer-Reported Delay != Provider-Caused Delay`

`Customer-Reported Damage != Provider-Caused Damage`

`Customer-Reported Loss != Provider-Caused Loss`

A customer-reported tracking problem is not automatically a tracking-system
failure or provider-performance fact.

`R4-U8 Complaint / Incident Feedback Semantics = UNRESOLVED`

Operational corroboration, where later available, remains separate evidence.

reinterpreted as customer-feedback verification.

Therefore:

`Evidence State != Feedback Verification`

`Source Relationship != Feedback Verification`

A future feedback-verification contract may need to distinguish bounded
states such as:

- author reference preserved;
- experience correlation present;
- experience verified;
- verification unknown;
- verification unavailable.

R4 does not establish the final verification vocabulary.

`R4-U9 Feedback Verification State = UNRESOLVED`

Feedback authenticity and abuse handling are also separate from experience
verification.

A person may have genuinely experienced a service while the submitted
feedback may still require separate authenticity or abuse evaluation.

Therefore:

`Verified Experience != Feedback Authenticity`

`Verified Experience != Feedback Truth`

R4 does not establish canonical duplicate, spam, manipulation, fraudulent
feedback or abuse-review semantics.

`R4-U10 Feedback Authenticity / Abuse Handling = UNRESOLVED`

## 21. Successor Boundary

R4 closes the bounded Provider Feedback Evidence & Verification research
scope.

A successor must consume the R1-R4 evidence boundaries without silently
reopening them.

R4 does not authorize provider scoring, ranking, recommendation or
selection.

The next B4-B research gate is:

`B4B-R5 — Provider Evidence Presentation & Customer Choice Boundary`

R5 may investigate how independently governed evidence families can be
presented to customers without collapsing them into one unsupported provider
quality score.

Candidate evidence families include:

- provider claims;
- capability evidence;
- promised SLA evidence;
- observed operational evidence;
- derived operational-performance evidence where separately established;
- customer feedback evidence;
- feedback verification state;
- provenance;
- freshness;
- completeness;
- uncertainty;
- cold-start / low-evidence state.

R5 should investigate explainable customer-facing presentation such as:

- what the provider says it can do;
- what SLA it promises;
- what operational evidence has been observed;
- what customers report;
- whether customer experience is verified;
- how much evidence exists;
- how recent the evidence is;
- what remains unknown or uncertain.

R5 must preserve:

`Provider Claim != Proven Performance`

`Promised SLA != Observed Outcome`

`Operational Performance Evidence != Customer Feedback`

`Customer Feedback != Provider Performance`

`Evidence Availability != Provider Quality`

`Missing Evidence != Negative Evidence`

`Low Evidence != Poor Provider`

R5 must not establish, without a separate governed lifecycle:

- provider score;
- provider rank;
- provider recommendation;
- provider selection;
- production schema;
- implementation authority.

This preserves a path for incumbent and newer providers to present
evidence-backed strengths without treating popularity or historical feedback
volume as provider quality by itself.
