## Controlling objective

Advance the existing canonical Recommendation Engine and its owned ranking capability without creating a competing engine, reviving retired Ranking V8, repeating resolved duplicate-ranking work, or absorbing upstream domain ownership.

## Lifecycle stages

### F0 — Canonical Runtime and Legacy Surface Classification

Read-only classification of every admitted Recommendation/Ranking production surface, test surface, runtime entrypoint, consumer, compatibility path, and legacy implementation generation. F0 must produce exact ownership and disposition evidence before any implementation wave is designed.

### F1 — Canonical Contract Baseline

Establish separately authorized tests for the preserved MA-2026-032 architecture: parser, policy, context, six-axis availability-aware scoring, ranking, provider orchestration, deduplication, reason construction, and `RecommendationResult`.

### F2 — Legacy Surface Disposition and Convergence

For each legacy surface, decide separately whether it is active canonical dependency, bounded compatibility adapter, migration candidate, or retired artifact. Ranking V7 and older recommendation/score engines may not be changed or removed until exact call-site and contract evidence is established.

### F3 — Canonical Runtime Composition

Converge authorized recommendation preparation, scoring, ranking, provider orchestration, and result composition onto the canonical package through bounded production waves. Preserve one ranking execution and one priority-sort execution where those contracts remain applicable.

### F4 — Consumer Alignment

Align only proven direct consumers: recommendation pipeline, API endpoints, generator compatibility, and UI/Experience adapters. Preference evidence may be consumed but Preference ownership remains independent.

### F5 — Integrated Verification and Completion Readiness

Verify canonical Recommendation behavior, selected consumers, preserved upstream boundaries, regression, retired-surface non-reintroduction, and completion readiness under separate execution authority.

## Preserved boundaries

- `32_RECOMMENDATION_ENGINE` owns recommendation scoring and ranking policy.
- Food Intelligence owns food-quality evidence and domain semantics.
- Market Intelligence owns market evidence.
- Marketplace Core owns marketplace truth and normalized marketplace inputs.
- Product Identity and Price Intelligence retain their canonical ownership.
- Preference and Experience remain independent consumers or upstream evidence owners.
- Cross-Border recommendation integration remains preserved and outside modification unless a later bounded subwave proves a necessary change.
- Ranking V8 remains retired.
- Resolved duplicate ranking and priority-sort execution remains resolved.

## Governance sequence

Each stage requires its own read-only preflight or exact-scope decision, then any necessary test or production write authority, bounded implementation, verification, and sealed review. No stage inherits write or execution authority from this decision.

## Exclusions
