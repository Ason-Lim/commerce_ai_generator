   paths required to make those contracts implementable;

   authorized test-first and implementation operations;
6. exact selected regressions and collection boundaries, without executing them;
7. whether fixtures or resources are explicitly excluded from Foundation; and
8. the next separately authorized one-use write step.

The decision may name prospective paths and obligations but cannot create,
modify, stage, commit, tag, push, test, or implement any of them.

- Foundation is limited to domain contracts, parser-model behavior, boundary
  behavior, and the minimum test-first structure required to prove them.

  registration, runtime routing, and full-suite execution remain later subwaves.

- Sauces remains P1, deferred, unallocated, and unreserved.
- Soy sauce, doenjang, gochujang, salt, and vinegar retain existing ownership.
- Alias Resolution and `Provider.aliases` remain sealed and unchanged.
