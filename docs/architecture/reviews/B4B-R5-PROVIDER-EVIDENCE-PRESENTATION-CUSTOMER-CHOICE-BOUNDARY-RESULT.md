## 8. Provenance / Freshness / UNKNOWN Boundary

### Provenance

Provenance may identify where evidence came from and when it was retrieved or
effective.

It does not establish truth.

`Provenance != Verification`

`Provenance != Truth`

A subject-supplied relationship must not automatically imply falsehood.

A third-party relationship must not automatically imply independence,
correctness, verification or authority.

### Freshness

Freshness may describe evidence recency under its canonical freshness
authority.

`Freshness != Quality`

`Freshness != Truth`

STALE evidence may be disclosed as stale.

STALE must not become a negative provider-quality judgment.

### UNKNOWN

UNKNOWN is an explicit evidence state.

`UNKNOWN != Zero`

`UNKNOWN != False`

`UNKNOWN != Verified Absence`

Unknown, unavailable and unresolved information should remain visible when
material rather than being silently converted into negative evidence.
