1. Purpose

This Architecture Reference Notice formally designates the Sprint 3 Domain Completion and Integration Verification lifecycle, as reproduced through the Coffee, Cheese, Wine, and Tea Knowledge Domains, as the official reference process for all remaining Sprint 3 Food Knowledge domains.

The purpose of this notice is to preserve architectural consistency, reproducibility, and evidence traceability while avoiding unnecessary redesign during Sprint 3.
