# 1. Purpose

This Architecture Notice announces the official adoption of the approved Sprint 3 Architecture Review Governance Model.

The purpose of this notice is to ensure that all remaining Sprint 3 Knowledge Domains follow a consistent Architecture Review process based upon independently verified evidence, progressive maturity, and clearly separated governance responsibilities.

This notice becomes effective immediately.

Responsible for:

Architecture Completion shall remain under the exclusive review authority of 00_1 Master Architecture.

---

# 5. Mandatory Governance Rules

Effective immediately:

1. Domain implementation shall not directly imply Architecture Completion.

2. Domain Integration Verification shall not imply Project-level Integration Completion.

3. Domain IVC documents shall declare Domain Integration only.

4. Architecture Completion shall require OAA, AVCR, and MACR.

5. Project-level Integration Completion shall remain under the authority of 99_Integration Verification Authority.

---

# 6. Architecture Observation Policy

Architecture Observations:

* shall be independently documented;
* shall distinguish verified facts from assumptions;
* shall not be treated as implementation defects unless evidence demonstrates direct attribution;
* may be deferred to a future Sprint when outside the approved Sprint scope.
